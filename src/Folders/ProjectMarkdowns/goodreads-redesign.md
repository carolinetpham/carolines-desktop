## Goodreads Redesign  
**Independent UX Case Study | January 2025 – Present**  
**Focus:** Systems redesign, friction reduction, reflective reading experience

### Overview
Goodreads Redesign is an ongoing end-to-end UX case study exploring how thoughtful readers discover, track, reflect on, and evaluate books. The project reimagines core reading workflows to better support nuanced engagement rather than performance-driven ratings.

### UX Problem
Despite its popularity, Goodreads’ primary flows can feel fragmented and cognitively heavy. Users often experience friction when trying to:
- Discover books aligned with their interests  
- Track reading progress in a meaningful way  
- Write reflective reviews without high effort  
- Express nuanced feedback beyond a simple star rating  

The platform’s heavy reliance on numerical ratings and cluttered information architecture can discourage thoughtful interaction.

### My Role
- Conducted a full heuristic evaluation to identify usability breakdowns  
- Performed competitive analysis across StoryGraph, Fable, and adjacent media platforms  
- Mapped the primary journey: Add → Start Reading → Update Progress → Finish → Reflect → Rate  
- Identified friction points across discovery, reflection, and rating flows  
- Designed updated IA, interaction patterns, and scalable UI components  

### UX Strategy
- Simplified core task flows to reduce cognitive load  
- Reframed rating interactions to support expressive, lower-friction feedback  
- Designed reflection tools that feel personal rather than performative  
- Improved visual hierarchy to reduce clutter and improve scanability  
- Built a scalable component system to support long-term accessibility and consistency  

### Execution
- Annotated screenshots and friction analysis documentation  
- Information architecture restructuring  
- Wireframes and high-fidelity mockups of redesigned flows  
- Iterative refinement of navigation, rating UI, and shelf management patterns  
- Early-stage design system development for cohesive UI behavior  

### Outcome
The redesign establishes a clearer, more task-oriented reading experience centered on reflection and usability. It demonstrates systems-level UX thinking, research synthesis, and the ability to translate user pain points into cohesive interaction redesigns.
