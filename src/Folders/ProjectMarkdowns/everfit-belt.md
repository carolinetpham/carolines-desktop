## EverFit Belt  
**Speculative Product Design | ARTF 1250 – Designing Interactive Experiences**  
**Focus:** Inclusive design, embodied interaction, sustainable product thinking

### Overview
EverFit is a speculative adaptive belt concept designed to respond to changing body measurements over time. The project explored how physical prototyping and narrative design tools can inform inclusive, long-term product experiences.

### UX Problem
Traditional belts assume static body measurements. This creates friction for users whose bodies change over time and contributes to unnecessary replacement purchases. The design challenge was to reimagine the belt as a responsive, durable object rather than a fixed accessory.

### Design Intent
- Support fluctuating body measurements without manual readjustment  
- Reduce overconsumption through a “buy once, use for years” model  
- Design for comfort, dignity, and inclusivity  
- Explore embodied interaction in a wearable object  

### My Role
- Researched historical and contemporary belt mechanisms  
- Defined a target user through persona and storyboard development  
- Built and iterated three physical prototypes  
- Integrated a speculative EEG-inspired sensing concept  
- Designed a supporting Figma web concept to communicate product positioning  

### Iterative Prototyping Process
**Prototype 1:**  
Closed-loop construction revealed a fundamental usability failure — highlighting the importance of validating mechanical function before aesthetic refinement.

**Prototype 2:**  
Mechanically viable but visually underdeveloped. Sensor integration concept lacked clarity in form and communication.

**Prototype 3 (Final):**  
Improved usability, clearer adaptive “sensor” integration, and refined visual direction (black + gold palette). Balanced functional clarity with product storytelling.

### Key UX Learnings
- Physical usability must be validated before visual polish  
- Narrative artifacts (personas + storyboards) sharpen product framing and feature decisions  
- Inclusive design means designing for change over time, not static measurements  
- Sustainability can be approached through longevity and adaptability, not just material choice  

### Outcome
Produced a refined physical prototype and companion web concept demonstrating systems thinking across object design, interaction logic, narrative framing, and sustainability strategy.
